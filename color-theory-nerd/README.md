# Color Theory Nerd — Peter T. Donahue

This directory collects research notes on **Peter T. Donahue**, who publishes as **Color Nerd** on YouTube and other platforms.

## Identification

I am confident this is the person intended by the original description:

- Peter Donahue calls himself an **artist and color theory educator** whose teaching leans heavily on **perception science** and color literacy.
- The Colour Literacy Project describes him as a **painter, graphic designer and educator**, known for video lessons on color perception, light and pigment mixing, and color harmony.
- He is a full-time art teacher at Morristown-Beard School and has lectured at universities and delivered color-theory keynotes and workshops.
- His Drawing America courses include actual acrylic/oil-paint exercises, pigment-specific palettes, live mixing demonstrations, and courses on the palettes of Caravaggio, Turner, Mary Cassatt, and Munsell.

That combination — Peter, teacher/lecturer, painter, perception, and hands-on mixing — is unusually specific and matches the recollection closely.

## Scope and method

This is a **curated research index, not yet an exhaustive scrape of every upload** on the channel. It emphasizes videos for which a title, URL, description, transcript-derived notes, or another reliable source could be checked. Summaries here are paraphrases, not copied transcripts.

Some of Donahue's short videos intentionally use simplified models to make a perceptual point. Where a claim is better treated as a teaching model than as a complete scientific statement, these notes say so. The aim is to preserve both what Donahue is trying to teach and the distinction between pedagogy, historical argument, and formal color science.
