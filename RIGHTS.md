# Rights boundary

## Current result

I have **not** found a Josef Albers painting whose underlying artwork is clearly released under a public-domain or open-content license.

The Josef and Anni Albers Foundation states that it holds copyright in **all works of art and all writings** of Josef and Anni Albers, and that permission must be obtained before reproducing either works or writings:

https://www.albersfoundation.org/rights

For this repository, do not treat any of these as equivalent:

1. a museum object record being public;
2. museum metadata being CC0;
3. a JPEG being downloadable;
4. the photograph of an artwork having a Creative Commons license;
5. the underlying Albers artwork itself being licensed for reuse.

Only (5), or another clear authorization covering reproduction of the artwork, is enough to commit an Albers image as reusable source material.

## Examples checked

### The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Several *Homage to the Square* records explicitly state copyright / Artists Rights Society restrictions. Example:

https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/488725

The Met has a broad Open Access program, but this Albers work is explicitly outside the public-domain image set.

### Smithsonian American Art Museum

*Homage to the Square—Insert* is downloadable on the museum site, but the Smithsonian collection record says **Usage Conditions Apply**:

https://www.si.edu/object/saam_1969.47.51

Therefore `Download` is not evidence of an open license.

### Aichi Prefectural Museum of Art

The collection system explains that images specifically labeled `Public Domain` or `CC0` may be reused, but it also warns that third-party copyright may remain and is the user's responsibility. The Albers object page does not provide enough evidence to clear the underlying Albers copyright:

https://jmapps.ne.jp/apmoa2/det.html?data_id=5296

### Wikimedia Commons

A photograph showing Albers *Studies for Homage to the Square* is licensed CC BY 2.0 as a **photograph**:

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Josef_Albers%27_Studies_for_Homage_to_the_Square.jpg

That license does not by itself establish permission to reproduce the copyrighted Albers works depicted in the photograph.

## Safe default for this repository

Until an image has an explicit rights chain covering the underlying Albers artwork:

- store links and factual metadata;
- make our own diagrams of general color-perception phenomena rather than copying a specific Albers composition;
- quote Albers only briefly and otherwise paraphrase with a precise source;
- preserve the distinction between an open museum API and an open artwork.
