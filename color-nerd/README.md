# Color Nerd — per-video archive

This directory is the source-by-source archive for **Peter T. Donahue / Color Nerd** (`@ColorNerd1`).

The sibling [`color-theory-nerd/`](../color-theory-nerd/) directory is for synthesis by topic. This directory is deliberately different: **one video at a time, preserving every substantive claim, demonstration, example, distinction, historical attribution, caveat, and reference that can be recovered.**

The goal is exhaustive notes, not a highlight reel.

## Capture rule

For each video, record:

1. canonical URL and video ID;
2. title, date, and length when recoverable;
3. transcript/source status;
4. a chronological claim ledger following the video's order;
5. physical demonstrations, diagrams, swatches, pigments, color spaces, historical systems, named people, books, papers, and tools;
6. definitions and distinctions Donahue makes;
7. places where he explicitly rejects a common classroom rule or color-wheel convention;
8. practical painting/design advice;
9. scientific or historical qualifications that matter for reading the explanation correctly;
10. links into the Albers work when the video bears on context, simultaneous contrast, perceptual equivalence, lightness/chroma, gamut, color ordering, or material reproduction.

Do not silently replace Donahue's teaching model with a different one. Preserve what he is saying first; add technical qualification separately.

## Copyright / transcription boundary

These are research notes and detailed paraphrases, not verbatim transcript mirrors. Short exact phrases can be retained when terminology matters, but the archive should capture the full intellectual content in our own words rather than reproducing entire copyrighted transcripts.

## Status vocabulary

- **full-note** — enough source/transcript material was available to recover the video's substantive content in sequence.
- **substantial-note** — major claims and demonstrations are captured, but some sequence/detail remains to recover.
- **catalog** — title/topic/URL known; needs transcript-level pass.
- **missing** — channel upload identified but not yet summarized.
